In the [last post](../building-a-level-builder/), I described our aspirations for a level builder for our next game, promising an update in the following "weeks or months."

Well today I'm back, and... goodness, what _year_ is it?!  So much has happened over the past 17 months despite only working on this part-time:  The game is now officially called [Delivery Derby](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4615650/Delivery_Derby/?snr=1_5_1100__1100&utm_source=fps-website) and you wishlist it on Steam.   We've significantly fleshed out the gameplay and design, got some music tracks [professionally produced](https://www.matteopalmermusic.com/), showed a demo at [2D Con 2026](https://www.2dcon.net/indie-games/indie-island/), and have professional game art in the works.

But more on that some other time.  Today, I just want to share the progress I've made on level editor tooling.  It has been quite a journey!
